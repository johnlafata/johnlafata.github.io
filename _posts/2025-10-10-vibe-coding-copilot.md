"Vibe-coding" gets described as a vibe for a reason — it feels less like writing code line-by-line and more like describing what you want to a pair programmer who never gets tired, has read millions of repositories, and costs a fraction of a human developer's time.

What changed my opinion of GitHub Copilot specifically was the multi-model approach: being able to switch models depending on the task, rather than being locked into one, turned out to matter more for day-to-day cost-effectiveness than any single model's benchmark score.
