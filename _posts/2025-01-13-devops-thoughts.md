The appeal of serverless isn't just that someone else runs the servers — it's that developers stop thinking about acquiring infrastructure for every new project, and scale-to-zero means idle capacity stops costing anything. That shift is most of what this note is about, with AWS Copilot as a concrete example of how fast a CI/CD pipeline to ECS/Fargate can come together when it's built on Infrastructure as Code and GitOps from the start.
