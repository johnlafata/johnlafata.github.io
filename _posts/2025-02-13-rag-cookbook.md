Most RAG write-ups stop at "connect an LLM to a vector store." This cookbook goes a step further with a handful of small, real applications built to exercise different shapes of the pattern: an account-planning assistant, a document-processing pipeline, and a product conversational knowledgebase, each with its own architecture notes.
